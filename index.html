The problem

Most athletes don't have a development plan. They have training sessions.

Youth athletes often work with a school coach, an AAU coach, a private trainer, a strength coach, and a parent — sometimes all in the same month.

Each of them sees a different part of the athlete's development. There's rarely one shared system connecting those observations and goals.

TrainerIQ creates one shared development plan around the athlete.

Turn every training session into progress.

Assessment

A baseline assessment scores Jordan Williams across decision-making, ball handling, and finishing — the starting point every plan is built from.

Development plan

TrainerIQ turns the lowest-scoring priorities into a 30-day focus: left-hand finishing, pick-and-roll reads, change of pace.

Session

Every session is planned around the current priorities — not a generic workout, a session built for this athlete's plan.

Observation

A 60-second post-session note — "struggles making the second read out of pick-and-roll" — is logged and attached to the plan.

Update

The plan adapts: decision-making under pressure moves up in priority, and next week's sessions shift with it.
